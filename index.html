Introduction

Pet owners cannot always monitor their pets when they are away from home. Computer vision can help detect pets, track their movement, recognize behaviors, and identify when they enter restricted areas.

This tutorial explains how object detection, object tracking, spatial analysis, and behavior recognition can work together to create a pet monitoring system.
